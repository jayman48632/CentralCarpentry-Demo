Our services

From the first post in the ground to the last piece of trim, one crew handles the job.

  • New Construction

    Homes, shops, and garages built from the footings up.

  • Renovation

    Additions, remodels, and repairs that match what's already there.

  • Pole Barns

    Post-frame buildings for storage, livestock, equipment, and shops.

  • Framing

    Walls, floors, and roof systems that are square, level, and plumb.

  • Flooring

    Hardwood, subfloors, and installs done right the first time.

  • Steel Roofing

    Ribbed and standing-seam steel that sheds Michigan snow.

  • Windows

    New and replacement windows, trimmed and sealed tight.

  • Siding

    Vinyl, steel, and wood siding for homes, barns, and outbuildings.

About us

Built the way it should be

Central Carpentry is a family-run Amish crew based just outside Clare. We build pole barns, homes, and everything in between for neighbors across mid-Michigan.

We keep things simple: a clear price, solid materials, and careful work. The same people who quote your job are the ones who build it.
